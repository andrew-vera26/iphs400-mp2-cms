⏺ Your DJI mic has an Auto Power Off feature. With it on, the transmitter shuts
  itself down after about 15 minutes without a connection to the receiver and
  without recording on its own. Once it's off, you have to press its power
  button again, which is what you just did.

  Turning it off (DJI Mic 2 or Mic 3, whose receiver has a touchscreen):
  1. On the receiver, swipe down from the top of the screen.
  2. Open Transmitter Settings → Transmitter Auto Off and set it to OFF.
  3. Also check the receiver's own settings for an auto power-off option and
     turn that off too.

  With auto-off disabled on both, neither will shut down on its own.

  DJI Mic Mini: the receiver has no screen, so change these settings in the DJI 
  Mimo app instead.

  Still expect a short power-saving sleep. This can't be turned off. If the
  transmitter loses the receiver's signal, for example while the MacBook is
  asleep, it goes into a light sleep and reconnects by itself once the signal
  returns. That doesn't need a button press, but the first second or two of
  dictation after a pause may be lost while it reconnects.

  Tradeoff: with auto-off disabled, a transmitter you forget about will run its
  battery down. Switch it off by hand, or put it back in the charging case, when
  you're done.

  Sources:
  - DJI Mic Series Intelligent Features Overview (DJI Support)
  - DJI Mic Mini 2 / Mic 2 Auto Power Off (DJI Support)
  - Disabling Mic 2 Auto Power Off (DJI Forum)
  - DJI Mic Mini power-saving and auto shutdown (heliguy)
  - DJI Mic 3 FAQ
