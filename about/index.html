About the club

Kenyon Model United Nations is a club at Kenyon College where students debate international politics and practice diplomacy. Our debate format follows the United Nations. The club has around 30 members and travels once or twice a semester to compete. We have been to Chicago and Ithaca. We also host our own conference at Kenyon, KokoMUN, and invite other college Model UN teams from Ohio.
